┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WHAT HAPPENS AUTOMATICALLY:                                             │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    1. MODULE_REGISTRY_LIST macro expands in module_manager.c
       ↓
    2. module_list[] is populated at compile time
       ↓
    3. When module connected, ADC reads identification voltage
       ↓
    4. Manager iterates through module_list[]
       ↓
    5. Finds match by comparing voltage to ident_mv_min/max
       ↓
    6. Calls matched module's start_function()
       ↓
    7. Your module is now running!
